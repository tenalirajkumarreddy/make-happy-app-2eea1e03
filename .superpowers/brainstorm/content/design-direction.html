What design personality fits the agent app?

Field agents use this in bright sunlight, on bumpy roads, at store counters. The tone should feel professional but not corporate, efficient but not cold.

A

Industrial / Utility

High-contrast, monochrome-dominant, orange/amber accent. Bold typography. Feels like a tool, not a website. Think work glove aesthetic — sturdy, no-nonsense.

Pros

  • Excellent sunlight readability
  • Fits "field agent" context naturally
  • Distinctive — no B2B app looks like this

Cons

  • Could feel too austere
  • May not match corporate brand
B

Vibrant / Energetic

Warm accent (teal/emerald/cyan) with deep navy base. Rounded corners, playful micro-interactions, subtle gradients. Feels alive — the app smiles at you.

Pros

  • Inviting and pleasant to use daily
  • Good contrast with warm accents
  • Memorable / brandable

Cons

  • Less "serious" vibe
  • Could feel generic if not executed well
C

Premium / Refined

Neutral base, deep indigo/charcoal, single bold accent (amber or gold). Generous whitespace, subtle shadows, refined typography (e.g. Sora or DM Sans). Feels expensive.

Pros

  • Elevates perceived app value
  • Professional, trustworthy
  • Works well for enterprise sales

Cons

  • May feel too formal for field work
  • Whitespace reduces info density
